Plant Scan ("the app") is provided by [[YOUR FULL NAME OR BUSINESS NAME]] ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app collects, why, and who it is shared with. It covers the Android app published as com.ongama.plantscan.

You do not need an account to use Plant Scan. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or location.

What we collect and why

DataWhyWhere it goes
Photographs you take or select To identify the plant in the picture Sent over an encrypted connection to our server, which forwards it to Google's Gemini API for analysis. We do not keep a copy.
A random installation identifier To count your free daily scans and recognise an active subscription Generated on your device and sent with each request. Stored on our server alongside your scan count.
Purchase token, if you subscribe To confirm with Google that a subscription is genuine and active Sent to our server, which checks it with the Google Play Developer API.
Advertising identifier To show ads in the free version Collected by Google AdMob, not by us. Subscribers see no ads.
Plants you save, and their photos So your saved plants are there next time Stored only on your device. We cannot see them.

The installation identifier is a random string created on first launch. It is not your device's serial number, advertising ID, or any other identifier that follows you between apps, and reinstalling the app produces a new one.

Who processes your data

  • Google (Gemini API) analyses the photographs you submit. See the Google Privacy Policy. Note that on the service tier we currently use, Google may use submitted content to improve their services.
  • Google AdMob serves ads in the free version and may use your advertising identifier to do so. See how Google uses data in advertising.
  • Google Play handles all payments. We never see your card details.
  • Cloudflare hosts our server, which passes your photos through and stores your scan count.

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with anyone beyond the processors listed above.

How long we keep it

Photographs are not stored on our server — they are forwarded for analysis and discarded once a result comes back. Your scan count and subscription record are deleted automatically 90 days after you last use the app. Anything saved in "My plants" stays on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app.

Your choices

  • Delete everything on your device by uninstalling the app, or remove individual plants by swiping them away in "My plants".
  • Reset your advertising identifier in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads, or opt out of personalised ads there.
  • Ask us to delete your server record by emailing [[CONTACT EMAIL]]. Because we hold no account details, we cannot find your record without the installation identifier, so please include it — you will find it in the app under [[WHERE, IF YOU ADD THIS]]. Uninstalling also causes the record to expire within 90 days.
